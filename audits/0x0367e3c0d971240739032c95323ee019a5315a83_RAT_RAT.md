#### 🟢 [L-1] Deployer holds 100% of supply (centralization / distribution risk)

**Description:**
The entire supply is minted to `msg.sender` in the constructor with no vesting, lock, or distribution logic.

```solidity
uint256 scaledSupply = totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_));
_mint(msg.sender, scaledSupply);
```

**Impact:**
The deployer controls 100% of tokens and can dump the entire supply at any time, creating rug-pull / liquidity risk for holders. This is inherent to fixed-supply tokens but should be disclosed.

**Location:**
Constructor of `CheesePadStandardToken`.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Publicly disclose the initial distribution and consider locking or vesting the deployer's allocation (e.g. a timelock or LP-lock) to build holder trust.

#### 🟢 [L-2] Unbounded multiplication can revert on extreme inputs

**Description:**
`totalSupply_ * (10 ** decimals_)` is unchecked-by-context arithmetic that will revert if the product overflows `uint256`.

```solidity
uint256 scaledSupply = totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_));
```

**Impact:**
Only a deployment-time revert (no runtime security impact), but a caller passing an extremely large `totalSupply_` will fail deployment with a non-descriptive panic instead of a clear error.

**Location:**
Constructor of `CheesePadStandardToken`.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Optionally add an explicit sanity cap / `require` on `totalSupply_` with a clear revert message. Low priority since ^0.8.20 already prevents silent overflow.

**Location:**

**💡 Recommendation:**
