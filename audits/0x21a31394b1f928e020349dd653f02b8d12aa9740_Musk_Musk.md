### Medium Findings

#### 🟡 [M-1] Entire Supply Minted to Deployer (Supply Centralization)

**Description:**
The full token supply is minted to the deploying EOA with no distribution logic, vesting, or lock.

```solidity
uint256 scaledSupply = totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_));
_mint(msg.sender, scaledSupply);
```

The deployer holds 100% of tokens at launch and can move or sell any portion at will.

**Impact:**
Concentrated ownership enables a rug pull / dump: the deployer can drain liquidity or crash price. Holders must fully trust off-chain distribution promises.

**Location:**
Constructor, `_mint(msg.sender, scaledSupply)`.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Verify on-chain distribution of the deployer balance (liquidity, vesting contracts, multisig). Prefer minting to a timelock/vesting contract or distributing per a published, verifiable allocation. Buyers should confirm deployer wallet holdings and LP lock status before trusting the token.

**Description:**
`totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_))` is checked arithmetic (Solidity ≥0.8), so an extreme `totalSupply_` reverts rather than overflowing silently, but there is no explicit cap or sanity bound on supply.

```solidity
uint256 scaledSupply = totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_));
```

**Impact:**
No fund loss; a deployer could set an arbitrarily large or trivially small supply. Only a deployment-time concern.

**Location:**
Constructor scaled-supply computation.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Optionally validate `totalSupply_` against a reasonable maximum. Not required for correctness since checked math prevents overflow.


**Impact:**
No persistent state exists yet to exploit via reentrancy, and no tokens are minted before the call, so impact is negligible. A malicious/reverting receiver only blocks that deployment.

**Location:**

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Acceptable as-is. If desired, move `_mint` before the external call to strictly follow checks-effects-interactions, though there is no exploitable state here.
