### Medium Findings

#### 🟡 [M-1] Unbounded supply scaling can overflow and revert deployment

**Description:**
The constructor scales the raw supply by `10 ** decimals_` without validating the magnitude of `totalSupply_`.

```solidity
uint256 scaledSupply = totalSupply_ * (10 ** uint256(decimals_));
_mint(msg.sender, scaledSupply);
```

Because Solidity ≥0.8 checks arithmetic, a large `totalSupply_` combined with high `decimals_` will overflow `uint256` and revert. While this does not create a fund-loss vulnerability, in a factory context it fails only at deploy-time and any native fee already forwarded via `feeReceiver_.call` before mint could still leave the fee spent if ordering differed. Here the fee is sent before `_mint`, so an overflow revert reverts the whole transaction (fee returned), but the design still relies on transaction atomicity rather than explicit bounds.

**Impact:**
Deployment reverts for large inputs; no on-chain funds lost, but poor UX and no clear error for callers. If future modifications move the fee transfer after mint or make it non-atomic, fee loss becomes possible.

**Location:**
`CheesePadStandardToken` constructor, `scaledSupply` computation.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Validate `totalSupply_` against a sane maximum (e.g. `require(totalSupply_ <= 1e30, "Supply too large")`) so callers receive a clear revert reason, and keep all fee transfers strictly before minting to preserve atomicity.

### Low Findings

#### 🟢 [L-1] Fee transfer to arbitrary address before state completion (reentrancy surface)

**Description:**
The constructor performs a low-level native-value call to a caller-supplied address before `_mint` completes.

```solidity
(bool ok, ) = feeReceiver_.call{value: msg.value}("");
require(ok, "Fee transfer failed");
_mint(msg.sender, scaledSupply);
```

During construction the contract has no exploitable persistent state and reentrancy into a not-yet-deployed contract is not practically dangerous, but the external call precedes minting, violating checks-effects-interactions ordering as a general pattern.

**Impact:**
Negligible in this constructor-only context; flagged for pattern hygiene.

**Location:**
Constructor, `feeReceiver_.call`.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Perform the `_mint` before the external fee transfer, or document that the ordering is intentional and safe during construction.
