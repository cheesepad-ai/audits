### Critical Findings

#### 🔴 [C-1] Trading gate enables an indefinite honeypot / sell freeze

**Description:**
`_update` blocks all transfers unless `tradingEnabled` is true or the sender/receiver is the deployer or an exempted address. `tradingEnabled` can only be set to `true` (never reset), but it starts `false` and there is no timelock or guarantee it will ever be enabled. The owner can also selectively exempt only their own addresses.

```solidity
function _update(address from, address to, uint256 value) internal override {
    require(
        tradingEnabled || from == deployer || to == deployer || isExempt[from] || isExempt[to],
        "Trading not enabled"
    );
    super._update(from, to, value);
}
```

Because the deployer/exempt addresses can transfer while `tradingEnabled` is false, the deployer can add liquidity and sell into buyers while ordinary holders are frozen and unable to sell. Combined with never calling `enableTrading()`, this is a classic honeypot pattern.

**Impact:**
Ordinary buyers can be permanently prevented from transferring or selling tokens while privileged addresses trade freely — total loss of funds for holders.

**Location:**
`OCEAN._update` and `enableTrading`.

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Remove the permanent trading gate, or make trading irreversibly enabled shortly after launch with a hard, short timelock. Do not allow privileged addresses to transfer while trading is globally disabled. Consider renouncing exemption/ownership control after launch to prove non-honeypot behavior.

#### 🟠 [H-2] Dual privileged roles (owner and deployer) with no renouncement path for deployer

**Description:**
The contract has two overlapping privileged actors: `owner` (Ownable, transferable/renounceable) and `deployer` (immutable, set to `msg.sender`). The deployer retains permanent transfer bypass, `rescueBNB`, and `rescueToken` rights. Even if ownership is renounced, the deployer keeps privileges forever because `deployer` is immutable.

**Impact:**

**Location:**

**💡 Recommendation:**
> **Action Required:** Consolidate privileges under a single `onlyOwner` role, or document clearly that the deployer retains permanent powers. Avoid immutable privileged addresses that survive ownership renouncement.
